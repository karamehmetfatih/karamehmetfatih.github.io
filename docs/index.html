✿ M. Fatih Kara’s Leafy Homepage ✿

Hello, World Wide Web!!

I’m M. Fatih Kara 🌱 — a plant scientist working on disease resistance in lettuce, and on how that response can be read at the level of individual transcripts rather than whole genes. PhD researcher at the University of York, based in Ankara, Türkiye.

My work combines time-series transcriptomics, reference transcriptome construction and CRISPR-Cas9 genome editing to find and test candidate regulators of resistance to the necrotrophic fungi Botrytis cinerea and Sclerotinia sclerotiorum. Before moving into plant pathology and molecular biology, I worked in seed science on the longevity, storage and priming of vegetable seed.
