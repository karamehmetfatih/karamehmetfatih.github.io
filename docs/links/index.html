🎨 The official colour of this homepage

Lettuce GreenPANTONE 13-0324 TCX#BED38Ergb(190, 211, 142)

Every green on this site is that one Pantone chip, tinted for the panels and shaded for the sidebar, with radish red #C8395B, butter yellow #F0C040 and ink blue #1C4F7C as the accents. The whole set is declared as custom properties at the top of assets/geocities/geocities.css — change --lettuce there and the site follows.

Two neighborhoods ship with it: SalinasValley, the house theme, named for the Salad Bowl of the World; and GrowthChamber, which is what a controlled plant experiment looks like from the inside — a black box, artificial light, no daylight at all. The buttons are in the sidebar, and your choice is remembered in this browser.
